LITERATURE REFERENCES FOR THE CONSERVATIVE BINARY ANIMATION

The animation numerically evolves a selected conservative ADM Hamiltonian.
The references below supply the physics or provide related analytical and
numerical context. No animation or numerical dataset was copied from them.

PRIMARY SOURCES FOR THE IMPLEMENTED PHYSICS

1. Sashwat Tanay, Leo C. Stein, and Jose T. Galvez Ghersi
   Integrability of eccentric, spinning black hole binaries up to second
   post-Newtonian order (2021).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.06586
   Full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2012.06586

   Used for: canonical orbital and spin Poisson brackets, conservative
   Hamiltonian through 2PN, and the interpretation of conserved quantities.
   Relevant locations: Eq. (6); Eqs. (12)-(19); Sec. III after Eq. (20);
   Sec. IV for perturbative integrability at 2PN.
   Their effective spin is half the effective spin used in this animation.
   Their L is our orbital J; their J is our total J.
   Exact integrability of the truncated 1.5PN Hamiltonian must be
   distinguished from perturbative integrability through full 2PN.

2. C. Aykroyd, A. Bourgoin, and C. Le Poncin-Lafitte
   Hamiltonian normal forms for the post-Newtonian binary problem.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12204
   Version used: https://arxiv.org/html/2409.12204v2

   Used for: the nonspinning orbital Hamiltonian in the companion
   mathematical draft and comparison of its 1PN and 2PN coefficients.
   Relevant locations: Eqs. (33)-(34).
   This is not a source for generic spinning-binary trajectories.

3. Gerhard Schaefer and Piotr Jaranowski
   Hamiltonian formulation of general relativity and post-Newtonian
   dynamics of compact binaries (updated review, 2024).
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s41114-024-00048-7

   Used for: leading spin-orbit, mutual spin-spin, and spin-induced
   quadrupole Hamiltonians in the companion mathematical draft.
   Relevant locations: Eqs. (7.72)-(7.74).
   The self-spin terms in the animation use black-hole quadrupole values.

RELATED WORK AND READING

4. Gihyuk Cho and Hyung Mok Lee
   Analytic Keplerian-type parametrization for general spinning compact
   binaries with the leading order spin-orbit interaction (2019).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02927

   Relevance: analytical orbital motion and spin precession for generic
   spin directions and eccentricity; Secs. II-III.
   Scope: Eq. (2.1) retains Newtonian orbital motion and leading spin-orbit
   coupling. The authors deliberately omit the 1PN orbital correction.
   Our case 4 includes that correction. The animation does not implement
   their analytical solution; it integrates Hamilton's equations.

5. Michael D. Hartl and Alessandra Buonanno
   The dynamics of precessing binary black holes using the post-Newtonian
   approximation (2005).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0407091
   https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.71.024027

   Relevance: numerical conservative dynamics including spin-orbit,
   spin-spin, and mass-monopole/spin-induced-quadrupole interactions,
   for quasi-circular and eccentric configurations.
   This provides context for numerical studies; its code or trajectories
   were not used to generate this animation.

IMPLEMENTATION AND SCOPE

The seven-case selection, initial masses and spins, eccentric initial data,
RK4 integration, frame reconstruction, controls, and conservation diagnostics
are implementation choices for this illustration, not results attributed to
these papers. Numerical checks test the implementation for the chosen setup;
they do not establish the accuracy of the PN approximation to full general
relativity. The animation does not evaluate a one-cycle Magnusian.
